How to play

Get a table going

Tap Start a table, then Invite and send the link. Friends open it, type their name and they're in. Tap an empty seat to sit or move seats. Bots fill any empty seats up to the Bots setting, so you can practice alone.

Sweat flips

The board runs out first. The small blind flips one card, which plays with the board. Then each player flips until they beat the best hand so far, going round the table (back to the SB too). Run out of cards without beating it and you're out. The last hand standing wins, and cards nobody flipped are shown at the end with a dashed outline. Prefer no order? Set Flip order to free peeling.

Peeling

Tap your card to squeeze it. Drag any edge or corner to peel, press and hold near the middle to tear out a dot, or turn it sideways. Set aside saves a card for later and Slam it throws it down when you think you've got the nuts.

Hands

Omaha rules: exactly 2 of your cards plus 3 from the board, unless Hands is set to Any cards. Gold-raised cards are the ones playing.

Money and the jackpot

Set a wager per hand and an optional bad beat jackpot (progressive keeps growing until it hits). Standings show what you're up or down on flips and on the jackpot separately, and Menu → Settle up works out who pays whom.

If someone drops

The table keeps going. Another phone takes over hosting, and if the host leaves mid-hand anyone can tap Take over as host to keep the hand going. Leaving the app and coming back puts you right back in your seat. Standings are saved on your phone.
